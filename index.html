Dear Teachers,

Happy Teacher's Day 🌸

Some lessons are taught in classrooms. Yours are the ones that stayed — in how I think, how I write, how I see the world.

Thank you for always being there. Not just behind the desk, but beside me — on the days I understood everything, and on the days I understood nothing at all.

Thank you for trusting me. For handing me responsibilities a little heavier than I thought I could carry, and then watching, patiently, as I discovered that I could.

You were never only a teacher of subjects. You were the one I could talk to — about equations and chapters, yes, but also about doubt, about fear, about life. You made it safe to be a work in progress.

And thank you for constantly inspiring me. Every time I felt like stopping, I remembered something you said — and kept going.

Here is the part you should be proudest of: the wish you are reading right now — a 3D world, an envelope that opens, a song that plays, code that I wrote myself — exists because of you. You taught me to write. To think. To analyze. To build. The fact that I can send my gratitude to you through a website of my own making is your lesson, still living in me.

If a student you taught can do this, imagine how far your teaching will travel with everyone else you believed in.

With love and endless gratitude,

— Shuvam

P.S. — this entire website was written, animated and deployed by your student. Every petal is code. Every line is a thank-you.
